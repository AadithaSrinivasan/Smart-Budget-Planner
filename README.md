# 💰 Smart Budget Planner

A multilingual personal finance web app for Indian households, individuals and senior citizens. Track income and expenses, set savings goals, plan debt payoff, simulate unexpected costs and stay on budget, in 7 Indian languages.

![Smart Budget Planner screenshot](screenshots/home.png)

## 🎯 The Problem

Many middle-class Indian families find it hard to balance income and expenses. Money goes out on daily spending, subscriptions, insurance and loans, and little is left for savings or emergencies. Most budgeting apps are built for one kind of user and one language, so many people can't use them comfortably.

## ✨ Our Solution

Smart Budget Planner gives each user a **personalized budget plan** based on their own income and spending. It adapts to who is using it:

- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Families**: the head of the family sees the household's spending, and each member records their own expenses
- 🧑 **Bachelors / Spinsters**: a simple monthly savings plan
- 👴 **Senior citizens**: tracks medical expenses, health insurance and side income, with automatic savings

## 🌟 Features

- 📊 Personalized plan generated from your income and expenditure
- 🥧 Pie charts showing where your money goes
- 📝 Daily expense entry
- 🎯 Commitment-based saving (for example, set aside money for a subscription or bill due in 3 months)
- 🔥 Streaks and badges for sticking to your plan
- 🧮 What-if simulation for unexpected costs
- 💳 Debt payoff planner
- 🛟 Emergency fund tracker
- 📅 Month-end balance prediction and monthly comparison
